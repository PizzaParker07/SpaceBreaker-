**Tholin Sand** · **Tholin Mud** · **Tholin Deposit** · **Tholin** · **Titan Bedrock Ice**

A world with thick air (1.5 bar of nitrogen, no oxygen) under an orange haze: dunes of organic sand, methane lakes near the poles and slow, heavy methane showers.

Falls are gentle and an elytra glides far. **Tholin** is the key resource here.

**Creature:** Methane Skater

**Methane skaters** walk on the lakes.

**Creature:** Tholin Worm

The shy **tholin worm** burrows into the sand and now and then leaves tholin behind. It breeds with tholin sand.
