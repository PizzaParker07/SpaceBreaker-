The suit's backpack holds two tanks (three with the Extra Oxygen module). Two full large tanks keep you outside for half an hour.

> The times are for walking. Sprinting uses **1.5 ×** as much air, mining and fighting **1.25 ×**, standing still only **0.8 ×**; the thermal, heated and radiation suits use **1.1 ×**. The oxygen time (shown below 30 % or with the inventory open) and the turn-back warning average the last few seconds, so if you run, the time left shrinks.

## Filling tanks
Put a tank in the cradle of the {item spacebreaker:oxygen_filler} and power it. It also refills the maneuvering unit's gas.
- **On Earth:** it fills tanks from the natural air **for free**; it only needs power.
- **On airless or toxic worlds:** it fills only from **oxygen piped in**. The air of a base's pressurised room does not count.

| Source | 1 mB = | For a small tank |
|---|---|---|
| Oxygen gas | 2 breath ticks | 3,000 mB |
| Liquid oxygen | 16 breath ticks | 375 mB |

{recipe spacebreaker:oxygen_filler}

> On the Moon your oxygen source is the {item spacebreaker:regolith_heater}: it turns rock into oxygen. Pipe it to the filler. → [In-Situ Resources](isru)

>! There is no air on the Moon or in orbit. Always carry spare tanks.
