{recipe spacebreaker:oxygen_tank}

## Filling tanks
The {item spacebreaker:oxygen_filler} refills tanks **from breathable air**, using power. Put a tank in the cradle and power it. It also refills the maneuvering unit's gas.

{recipe spacebreaker:oxygen_filler}

> [!WARNING]
> There is no air on the Moon or in orbit. There the filler only works inside an oxygenated room. Always carry spare tanks.
