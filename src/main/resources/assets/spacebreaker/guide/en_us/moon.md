Airless, silent and grey. Gravity is **one sixth** of Earth's: you jump higher and fall slower.

> [!WARNING]
> There is **no air** on the Moon. Without a suit, or with an empty tank, you take damage every second. Carry spare tanks.

## Rocks
{items spacebreaker:lunar_regolith spacebreaker:fresh_regolith spacebreaker:mare_basalt spacebreaker:ilmenite_ore spacebreaker:anorthosite}
- **Regolith**: fine grey dust that keeps every boot print.
- **Fresh regolith**: the bright ejecta blanket around young craters; the Moon's rays and halos are made of it. It keeps boot prints too, digs with a shovel and counts as regolith in the sintering furnace and the ISRU machines.
- **Mare basalt**: the dark plains. Its **ilmenite** gives titanium and oxygen.
- **Anorthosite**: the bright highlands. Cut it in a stonecutter.

## Resources
{items spacebreaker:icy_regolith spacebreaker:lunar_ice spacebreaker:kreep_ore spacebreaker:meteoric_iron}
- **Ice** hides in the permanently dark floors of polar craters. Melt it for water.
- Those floors are **cold traps** at about **−233 °C**, day and night. The Mk1 suit cannot cope there: wear the heated suit. A message tells you when you step in and out. → [In-Situ Resources](isru)
- **KREEP** holds rare earths and thorium.
- **Meteoric iron** is left from old impacts and gives raw iron.

> [!TIP]
> Build your base on the rim of a polar crater: light for solar panels, ice inside.

## Eclipses
Seen from a moon, its planet passes in front of the Sun now and then. The Sun dims, and the planet's dark side is ringed by a thin red-orange band of all its sunrises and sunsets at once. Light and solar panel output drop, and the day's warmth fades slowly toward the night temperature. Mission control warns you about a minute ahead. **/spacebreaker eclipse** tells you when the next one comes.

> Panels go dark during an eclipse, so keep your batteries full. → [Night and power](power#night)

## Night sky
On the near side, Earth lights the lunar night: under a full Earth the ground glows a faint blue-white; under a crescent it is nearly dark. On the far side Earth never rises. Now and then, just after sunset or before sunrise, a thin glowing band hugs the horizon above the hidden Sun: dust lifted by static charge, rare and brief.

## Volcanic domes
In places steep, smooth domes of light anorthosite rise from the plains: 45–95 blocks across, up to 28 high, with a tiny pit on top.
