## The backpack
The {item spacebreaker:eva_chestplate} carries the life-support pack. It holds **two tanks**: wear the suit and {key key.use} with a tank in hand. The tanks empty one after the other. → [Oxygen](oxygen)

## The helmet
The helmet display shows oxygen, suit status, body and outside temperature, radiation and gravity. If the Sun is blinding, lower the **gold sun visor**: {key key.sneak} + {key key.use} with the helmet in hand.

## Suit tiers
- **Mk1**: −180 °C to 130 °C, lets 70% of radiation through. For the Moon and orbit.
- **Mk2** (coming): wider range, less radiation, dust-proof. For Mars.
- **Mk3** (coming): hard shell, shielded. For Jupiter's moons.

Mk1 handles the lunar day and night. For harsher worlds research {tech spacebreaker:suit_mk2} and {tech spacebreaker:suit_mk3}.
