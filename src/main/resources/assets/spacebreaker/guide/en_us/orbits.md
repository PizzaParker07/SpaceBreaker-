In orbit you are still **falling**, but moving sideways so fast that the ground curves away beneath you.

| | |
|---|---|
| Low Earth orbit speed | ≈ 7.8 km/s |
| Launch cost (with losses) | ≈ 9.4 km/s Δv |
| Transfer to the Moon | ≈ 4.0 km/s Δv |

> [!NOTE]
> The difference is **drag** and **gravity losses**: Δv spent pushing through air and climbing straight up.
