| Where | Ruin |
|---|---|
| Mars | Abandoned research station: a ribbed dome hub with four pressure tubes (greenhouse, lab and ISRU control, a storm-breached garage, airlock), a buried solar array, propellant tanks and a drilling rig outside; an arrow trap in the garage link, and the last log on a sign |
| Mars | Buried ancient riverbed with a fallen probe |
| Mars | **Lost lander**: an octagonal lander on splayed legs, one snapped so it sags at that corner, one solar wing torn off in the dust, skid marks from the bounce, its heat shield on edge half buried nearby and its parachute spread over the dust. Rare (about one per kilometre); you need a [rover](rover). Its data recorder holds a disk, experiment data and parts. |
| Phobos | Crashed sample-return probe |
| Deimos | Deimos ice cache |
| Venus | Abandoned research aerostat → [Venus clouds](venus_clouds) |
| Mercury | Abandoned polar ice mine |

Their disks teach **methane engines**, the **Sabatier process** and **Martian geology**.

> On many small moons, asteroids and comets you will find a **crashed supply lander** at the end of its skid trench: a two-stage craft on its side, its descent stage with a burnt-out engine and a snapped leg, and in front the white ascent cabin. Step in through the airlock door at its nose: two seats face a dead console, and the cabin locker and the crew's last message wait inside. Burst cargo pods lie along the trench.

> Loot in some ruins, such as the Mars research station, waits in a {item spacebreaker:supply_locker}: a wall cabinet with 27 slots that fills the first time it is opened. It gives a comparator signal, and you can break it and take it home.

> [!TIP]
> You earn an advancement the first time you discover each ruin. Bring a sample kit: the ground around ruins gives good samples.
