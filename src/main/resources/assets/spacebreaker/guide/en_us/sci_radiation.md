On Earth the magnetic field and thick air protect you. In space and on airless worlds, charged particles from the Sun and the galaxy hit you directly.

## How much? {#dose}
Dose is measured in **mSv** (millisievert). On the surface, unshielded, per day:

| Place | Daily dose |
|---|---|
| Earth | ≈ 0.007 mSv |
| Mars | 0.67 mSv |
| Moon | 1.4 mSv |
| Callisto | 0.1 mSv: the safest of Jupiter's moons |
| Ganymede | 80 mSv |
| Europa | 5,400 mSv |
| Io | 36,000 mSv: deadly within minutes |

> [!WARNING]
> Jupiter's radiation belts are the most dangerous place in the Solar System. Do not land on Io or Europa without strong radiation shielding.

## How to stay safe {#shield}
- **Shielding**: the radiation shielding in your suit and the walls of your base cut the dose.
- **Ground**: a few metres of regolith is the best shield. Build your base half buried.
- **Time**: dose adds up. Keep visits to dangerous places short.
- **Air**: Titan's thick air stops almost all of it.
