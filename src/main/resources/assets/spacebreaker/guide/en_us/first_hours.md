The shortest way from a new world to your first rocket. Follow the link in each step for the details. → [The Road Ahead](progression)

## 1. Power
1. Combine a copper plate with shears to make **three copper wires** by hand.
2. Wire, redstone and an iron plate make a circuit; the circuit makes a {item spacebreaker:combustion_generator}. It burns coal or wood.
{recipe spacebreaker:copper_wire_by_hand}

## 2. Research
Put a {item spacebreaker:research_lab} next to the generator and collect Earth samples with a sample kit. Start with {tech spacebreaker:metal_pressing}, {tech spacebreaker:electrolysis}, {tech spacebreaker:cryogenics} and {tech spacebreaker:fuel_refining}. → [How Research Works](research_basics)

{page}
## 3. Liquid oxygen
1. Build the {item spacebreaker:electrolyzer} next to an **endless water source** and feed it with a pipe in pull mode: **100 mB/t**, no bucket runs.
2. **500 mB water → 500 mB oxygen** (and 1000 mB hydrogen).
3. {item spacebreaker:cryogenic_liquefier}: **1000 mB oxygen → 500 mB liquid oxygen**. → [Cryogenics](cryogenics)

## 4. RP-1
1. Find an oil seep; wet biomes have sparse fields too. Build a {item spacebreaker:pumpjack} on top of it.
2. {item spacebreaker:refinery}: **1000 mB crude oil → 500 mB RP-1** (plus 250 mB methane and a plastic sheet). → [Oil Refining](oil_refining)

{page}
## 5. How much fuel?
For a first rocket with one stage, a {item spacebreaker:kerosene_engine} and a {item spacebreaker:fuel_tank_large} (7800 kg of propellant), roughly:
| Fuel | Amount | Raw material |
|---|---|---|
| RP-1 | ~2700 mB | ~5400 mB crude oil |
| Liquid oxygen | ~4900 mB | ~9800 mB oxygen, so ~9800 mB water |

> Oxygen takes longest: build the electrolyser early and leave it running.

> Tanks keep their contents when broken: you can carry fuel to the pad in a tank.

## 6. The first rocket
Take the **Basic Rocketry** disk from the observatory, make the parts and build the pad. → [Abandoned Observatories](observatory), [Your First Flight](first_flight)
