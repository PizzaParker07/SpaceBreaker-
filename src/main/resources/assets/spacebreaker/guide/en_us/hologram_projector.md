Shows where everything goes when you build a large structure: it appears in the world as a **see-through hologram**.

{recipe? spacebreaker:hologram_projector}

## How to use it
1. {key key.use} with the projector in hand: the **blueprint list** opens. Pick a structure.
2. {key key.use} on a block: the hologram is placed next to it.
3. {key key.sneak} + mouse wheel: **turn** the hologram.
4. {key key.sneak} + {key key.use} (in the air): show it **layer by layer**. Start at the bottom and move on when a layer is done.
5. {key key.sneak} + {key key.use} (on a block): **remove** the hologram.

> The **Show hologram** button under the 3D structures in this book loads that structure straight into the projector in your hand.

Missing or wrong blocks stay visible in the hologram. A message tells you when the structure is complete.

## Blueprints
- [Launch Site](launch_pad): pad, flame trench, service tower, fuel umbilical and access arm.
- [Pressurised Habitat](base_air): a room you can walk around in without a helmet.
