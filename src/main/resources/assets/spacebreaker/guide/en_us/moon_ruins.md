## Abandoned Survey Outpost
Stands on the plains: a central module, a lab, a store or a collapsed greenhouse, a drone hangar and crew quarters, built from station blocks with blue window bands, ceiling LEDs, {item spacebreaker:control_desk} consoles and bunks. Sintered regolith berms line the walls; outside are a pipeline, an airlock with two iron doors, a solar field with broken panels, a striped landing pad and an antenna. **Survey drones** still guard it.
- Floor lights lead to the **vault**, but its power is cut: take the power cell from the command chest and fit it in the slot above the vault door.
- A pressure plate fires an arrow trap.

## Old Ice-Drilling Station
Found only on the permanently shadowed floors of polar craters, the cold traps: a drill tower with a chain drill string down into the ice, a ready-made control hut whose RTG still runs, with {item spacebreaker:control_desk} consoles and a cable down to the drill, and core racks. The racks hold an {item spacebreaker:ice_core_sample}, with luck an {item spacebreaker:rtg_fuel_pellet}, and research data. Melt the ice core for Moon ice.

>! Heat Siphons live in the cold traps too and drain the station's RTG. Go down in a heated suit. → [Life on the Moon](moon_life)

## Ancient Lava Tube
Opens through collapsed roofs in the lunar maria. Deep inside, down a winding tunnel, lie the wreck of a robotic lander (a gold-foiled descent stage, four legs with one snapped, its engine, dish and a torn-off solar wing, and a {item spacebreaker:supply_locker}) and the nest of an **Elder Regolith Tick**.

> [!NOTE]
> Their labs and probes keep disks for the next steps: life support, construction and the Moon itself.

> [!TIP]
> Bring ladders before you drop into a lava tube: climbing back out of the roof hole can be hard.
