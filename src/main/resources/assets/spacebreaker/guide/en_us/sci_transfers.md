You do not fly straight at a planet. The cheapest way is an ellipse that touches both orbits: the **Hohmann transfer**. Two burns are enough: one to leave, one to arrive.

## Earth to Mars {#mars}
1. Burn **3.6 km/s** in low Earth orbit.
2. Coast for **259 days** with the engines off.
3. Burn **2.1 km/s** to enter orbit at Mars (or brake in its air, see [Aerobraking](sci_atmosphere)).

## Launch windows {#window}
Mars keeps moving while you travel. To meet it, Mars must be **44° ahead** of Earth when you leave. That alignment only comes round every so often:

| Target | Trip | Window every |
|---|---|---|
| Moon | 5 days | always open |
| Venus | 146 days | 584 days |
| Mars | 259 days | 780 days (≈ 26 months) |
| Jupiter | 2.7 years | 399 days |
| Saturn | 6 years | 378 days |

> [!TIP]
> Time runs fast in the game: with the default setting a Mars trip takes about **1.7 real hours**, and Mars windows come about **5.2 real hours** apart. The star map and the launch console show how long until the next window.

## To moons {#moons}
Going from a planet to its own moon needs no window: the moon goes round in hours or days.
