Look at the land from above: folded tesserae, broad shield volcanoes and highlands coated in **metal frost** that condenses out of the hot air like snow.

{page}
## Into the clouds
As you climb into the cloud deck the air cools and thins. Near the cloud tops it is about **1 bar** and **room temperature**: the friendliest place in the solar system after Earth.

> [!WARNING]
> But the clouds are **sulfuric acid**. Your armour needs an acid coating. → [Life and Ruins in the Clouds](venus_clouds)

## The backwards planet
Venus turns backwards and very slowly: the sun **rises in the west**, and a day lasts months.
