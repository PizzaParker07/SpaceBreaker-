## Gravity {#gravity}
Gravity depends on a world's mass and radius.

| Earth | Moon | Mars | Titan | Phobos |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9.8 m/s² | 1.6 | 3.7 | 1.35 | 0.006 |

The realism setting softens this for play: on **Balanced** the Moon feels like ≈ 26 % of Earth's gravity (really 16.5 %).

## What air does {#air}
- **On the way up** drag costs Δv: about 130 m/s on Earth.
- **On the way down** it slows you for free. Your orbital speed turns into heat; you need a [heat shield](rocket_parts).
- **Parachutes** depend on how thick the air is: on Earth you come down at ≈ 8 m/s. Mars' air is very thin; parachutes are not enough and the last part is a powered landing (≈ 0.65 km/s).

## Aerobraking {#aerobraking}
Instead of burning to get into orbit when you arrive, you can skim the top of the atmosphere and let it slow you down.

| Arrival | With engines | With air |
|---|---|---|
| Mars orbit | 2.1 km/s | ≈ 0.02 km/s |
| Back to Earth | 0.8 km/s | ≈ 0 |

>! Aerobraking needs a heat shield. Diving into Venus' very thick air needs a stronger one.
