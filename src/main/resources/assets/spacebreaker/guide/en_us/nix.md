Nix and Hydra are two of Pluto's tiny outer moons: lumpy shards of water ice about 19 km across, where a jump almost reaches orbit. **Nix** is grey ice with a big reddish stain of tholin.

> {item spacebreaker:nix_ice} melts to water in an ice melter: you can refuel here.
