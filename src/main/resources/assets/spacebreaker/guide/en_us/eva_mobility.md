## Maneuvering unit
A cold-gas backpack. It fits onto an {item spacebreaker:eva_chestplate} (like a tank: wear the suit and {key key.use} the unit).
- {key key.jump}: up · {key key.sneak}: down.
- In zero gravity you move freely in six directions; on the Moon you can hover.
- A full unit flies for about **2 minutes**. Refill it at an {item spacebreaker:oxygen_filler}.

## Magnetic boots
Keep you on the floor in low and zero gravity. Press {key key.sneak} to let go.
