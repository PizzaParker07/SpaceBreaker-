New machines, rocket parts and suits are unlocked by **technologies**. You learn a technology in one of three ways:

1. **Field sample:** soil and rock from a world. Every world teaches different technologies.
2. **Tech disk:** an old data cartridge found in ruins. Each disk holds one technology.
3. **Experiment record:** data from flights and probes.

All three are analysed in a [Research Lab](research_lab).

> Every technology has a sample source, so you never depend on luck. The one exception is **Basic Rocketry**: its disk is always in an observatory archive. Disks are a shortcut: they teach that technology without waiting for samples.

## Taking samples
{recipe spacebreaker:sample_kit}

Hold the {item spacebreaker:sample_kit} and press {key key.use} on natural ground (stone, soil, sand, gravel, ore). The kit bottles a sample of the world you stand on. It lasts 32 uses.

{items spacebreaker:field_sample spacebreaker:experiment_data spacebreaker:tech_disk}

> [!TIP]
> Earth samples teach beginners the most basic technologies. Samples from the Moon, Mars or Venus reveal things you can only learn there.
