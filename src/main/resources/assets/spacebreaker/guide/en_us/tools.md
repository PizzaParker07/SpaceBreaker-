## Titanium tools
{items spacebreaker:titanium_pickaxe spacebreaker:titanium_axe spacebreaker:titanium_shovel spacebreaker:titanium_hoe spacebreaker:titanium_sword}
Between iron and diamond: **1,100 uses**, speed 7, iron mining level and a quicker swing. Repair them with titanium ingots. → [Metals and Alloys](metals_alloys)
{recipe spacebreaker:titanium_pickaxe}

## Electric drill
{items spacebreaker:electric_drill}
While charged, the {item spacebreaker:electric_drill} digs stone and soil faster than a diamond pickaxe and never wears out. It holds **100,000 FE** and uses **100 FE** per block. Charge it in the Suit Maintenance Station (in the slot or in your hand) or from any FE charger. → [EVA Suit](eva_suit)
{recipe spacebreaker:electric_drill}

## Environment sensor
{items spacebreaker:environment_sensor}
Use the {item spacebreaker:environment_sensor}: a ten-second card shows pressure, air composition, temperature (in sun or shade), radiation dose rate, gravity and the ground underfoot. Use it first whenever you land on a new world.
{recipe spacebreaker:environment_sensor}
