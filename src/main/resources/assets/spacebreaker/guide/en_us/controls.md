| What it does | Key |
|---|---|
| Open the research tree | {key key.spacebreaker.research} |

→ [Flight and Navigation](navigation)

## With the mouse
- **Gold sun visor:** hold {key key.sneak} and press {key key.use} with the {item spacebreaker:eva_helmet} in hand.
- **Fitting a tank:** {key key.use} with an {item spacebreaker:oxygen_tank} while wearing an {item spacebreaker:eva_chestplate}.
- **Putting the rocket on the pad:** {key key.use} the {item spacebreaker:rocket} on the middle of the flame trench.
- **Boarding:** {key key.use} the rocket. Press {key key.sneak} to get out.
- **Taking the rocket down:** hold {key key.sneak} and hit it.
- **Pipe pull mode:** hold {key key.sneak} and {key key.use} the end of a pipe.

## Settings {#settings}
Client settings live in the game's config folder:
- **spacebreaker-worlds-client.toml:** **planetStyle** sets how planets and moons look from outside: **VOXEL** (a ball of blocks, the default), **CUBE** (a slowly turning pixel cube) or **ROUND** (the old smooth disc). **bodyScale** sets how big the Sun and planets look (1 = real size; 8 by default). The style is used in the sky, below you in orbit and on the star map.
- **spacebreaker-hud.toml:** the helmet display, visor rim and sound. → [Helmet](eva_suit#hud)
