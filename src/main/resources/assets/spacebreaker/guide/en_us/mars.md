A cold desert under a butterscotch sky.
- **Air:** thin carbon dioxide, under one hundredth of Earth's pressure. You cannot breathe it.
- **Gravity:** 0.38 g.
- **Temperature:** nights fall far below freezing.

> [!WARNING]
> Oxygen alone is not enough here: you need a **sealed suit**. → [EVA Suit](eva_suit)

{items spacebreaker:martian_regolith spacebreaker:martian_sand spacebreaker:martian_basalt spacebreaker:martian_sandstone}
The north is a low, smooth plain; the south is old cratered highland. Look for the great volcanoes, the long canyons, the dune seas and the polar ice caps.

{page}
## Dust storms
Storms can wrap the whole planet for days.
- They dim the sun: **solar panels give much less power.**
- You cannot see beyond a few blocks.
- Dust-proof seals keep your machines and suit running.

> [!TIP]
> Don't rely on the sun in storm season: keep a fuelled generator or a full battery nearby.

## Blue sunsets
Fine dust scatters red light to the sides and lets blue pass straight on. Around a low sun the sky turns **blue**: the opposite of Earth.
