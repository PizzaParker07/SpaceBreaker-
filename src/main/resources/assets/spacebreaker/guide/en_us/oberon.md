The outermost of Uranus' large moons: old, heavily cratered reddish-grey ice. Most of its larger craters have floors flooded with {item spacebreaker:dark_crater_fill}. A mountain about **11 km** high stands west of the landing site, and **Mommur Chasma** cuts across the plains to the east.

> {item spacebreaker:oberon_ice} melts to water in an ice melter: water for your fuel.

{entity spacebreaker:rim_burrower}
**Rim burrowers** are squat, broad-clawed grazers. Hit one on loose ground and it digs itself in and cannot be hurt for a while; on hard ice it can only run. They breed on ammonia crystals.

## Ruins
A crashed supply lander holds a {tech spacebreaker:tectonic_mapping} disk.
