## Cloud Jelly
Drifts on sacs of light gas and sieves sulfur from the air. Harmless. It drops a {item spacebreaker:lift_gas_sac}: use it to fall slowly for 30 seconds.

## Acid Ray
Glides in wide curves. Leave it alone: it **sprays acid** that eats into armour. Drops sulfur and leather.

{page}
## Acid coating
{items spacebreaker:acid_coating}
Put any armour piece and an {item spacebreaker:acid_coating} together in a crafting grid: the armour becomes **acid-proof**.

## Floating platforms
{items spacebreaker:composite_panel spacebreaker:aerogel spacebreaker:envelope_fabric}
Light composite panels and aerogel let you build platforms that float in the breathable-air layer.
{recipe spacebreaker:composite_panel}

## Aerostat platform kit
{items spacebreaker:aerostat_kit}
The quickest way to a home in the clouds. Use an {item spacebreaker:aerostat_kit} in the **habitable cloud band** (about 1 bar and room temperature, from the cloud base to about 30 blocks above the cloud tops) and it inflates a floating base: a **9×9** composite deck with a safety railing, two gates and mooring cleats at the corners, and overhead a round two-tone envelope of lift gas with a grey equator ring, hung from a load ring with an LED lamp.

{recipe spacebreaker:aerostat_kit}

>! It only fills empty air and does not work below the band, where the envelope would cook, or above it, where it would not lift.

> Hunt cloud jellies for their lift gas sacs, craft the kit and set up your base in the clouds. Wear acid-coated armour.

## Venus descent probe
{items spacebreaker:descent_probe}
The surface (464 °C, 92 bar) is closed to people. Release a {item spacebreaker:descent_probe} from the cloud deck, an aerostat base for example: it sinks under its parachute, and at touchdown it sends you **2 Venus samples** and **Probe Landing** data, then falls silent from the heat within about a minute.
{recipe spacebreaker:descent_probe}

>! It can only be released on Venus from the cloud deck (y ≥ 175); lower down or anywhere else it is not used up. The samples go into your inventory, or drop at your feet when it is full. If you are offline when the probe lands, they do not arrive.

> Cloud jelly → aerostat kit → base in the clouds → descent probe → Venus samples → Venus technologies.

> [!NOTE]
> A derelict research aerostat floats above the cloud tops: a long envelope with fins and two engine pods over an octagonal gondola with a gold acid-proof band. Inside are a glazed bridge, crew berths, a lab with a research bench and an atmosphere processor, and supply lockers with tech disks, guarded by acid rays. The stern compartment has been eaten through by acid and sealed off; read the last log by the door.

## The balloon's puzzles
- There are three **gas valves**; only the one marked **sulfur yellow** opens the gondola door.
- The gangway has a **falling floor** trap; the net below catches you.
