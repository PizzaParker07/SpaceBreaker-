Three machines turn raw materials into rocket material.

> **Redstone control:** the button on every machine's screen switches between three modes: ignore redstone, run with a signal, or run without one.

## Crusher
Grinds raw ore into dust: one dust and a **50% chance of a second**.

{recipe spacebreaker:crushing/raw_bauxite}

## Electric arc furnace
Smelts dust and melts metals together into alloys. → [Metals and Alloys](metals_alloys)

{recipe spacebreaker:arc_smelting/steel}

## Press
Turns ingots into **plates**, **wires** and **rods**. The **die** you put in decides which; a die is never used up.

{items spacebreaker:plate_press_die spacebreaker:wire_press_die spacebreaker:rod_press_die}

{recipe spacebreaker:pressing/aluminum_plate}

> [!TIP]
> Every machine needs power. Link a few generators through a {item spacebreaker:battery_bank} and the power keeps flowing.
