A comet is a dirty snowball: water ice under a crust of dust darker than charcoal. As it nears the Sun the ice turns to gas, and a **coma** forms round the nucleus with a **tail** pointing away from the Sun. → [Asteroids](sci_asteroids)

| Activity | What happens |
|---|---|
| Dormant | Far from the Sun, the ice hardly sublimates |
| Weak | A thin coma |
| Active | A clear coma and tail |
| Great comet | A long tail shining across the sky |

> Activity follows the distance from the Sun: beyond about 3 AU the ice barely sublimates.

{page}
## Three comets
{items spacebreaker:comet_dust spacebreaker:comet_ice spacebreaker:comet_jet}
- **67P/Churyumov-Gerasimenko:** a dark, two-lobed nucleus a few kilometres long, its ice hidden under a crust of dust darker than charcoal.
- **Encke:** small, old and nearly worn out, baked every 3.3 years by its short orbit; a dusty, hummocky surface.
- **Halley:** the most famous one; a long, peanut-shaped nucleus blacker than coal, with strong jets.

> While the Sun is above the horizon, thin columns of gas and dust rise from open {item spacebreaker:comet_jet} blocks. They are **visual only** and cannot hurt you.

{page}
> **Hale–Bopp** and **Tempel 1** are in the catalogue too, but have no world to land on: they show only in the sky and on the star map. At perihelion Hale–Bopp is a great comet.

## Fuel
Comet dust is useful too: in the regolith heater one block gives **300 mB of water** and **100 mB of CO₂**, plus a lump of {item spacebreaker:tholin} 15% of the time. → [Asteroid mining](near_earth_asteroids#mining)
{recipe spacebreaker:regolith_processing/comet_dust}

All three have water ice: {item spacebreaker:comet_ice}, {item spacebreaker:encke_ice} and {item spacebreaker:halley_ice} melt to water in an ice melter. Split it into fuel by electrolysis: comets are **refuelling stops**. → [In-Situ Resources](isru)

>! There is almost no gravity. Come in slowly when you land; a jump can fling you all the way to orbit. Clip on a {item spacebreaker:safety_tether} if you need to.

## Ruins
Each has a crashed supply lander with a {tech spacebreaker:microgravity_mining} or {tech spacebreaker:spectroscopy} disk.

> Landing on a comet earns the **Ice Wanderers** advancement; Solar System Wanderer needs it too.
