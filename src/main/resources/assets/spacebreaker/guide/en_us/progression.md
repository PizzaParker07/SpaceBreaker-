The road to the Moon and on to Mars, step by step:

1. **Find bauxite.** Look for red-brown {item spacebreaker:bauxite_ore} in the stone of warm hills. Smelt it into {item spacebreaker:aluminum_ingot}. → [Ores](ores)
2. **Take samples.** Make a {item spacebreaker:sample_kit} and use it on natural ground. → [How Research Works](research_basics)
3. **Build a lab.** A {item spacebreaker:research_lab} needs aluminium plates and a little copper. Put a {item spacebreaker:combustion_generator} next to it. → [Research Lab](research_lab)
4. **Learn your first technologies.** Earth samples teach basics such as Aluminium Smelting, Metal Pressing, Electronics and Solar Power.
5. **Grow your industry.** Work metal with the {item spacebreaker:crusher}, {item spacebreaker:arc_furnace} and {item spacebreaker:press}; make circuits and parts in the {item spacebreaker:fabricator}. → [Processing](processing)
6. **Find the observatory.** The cellar of the abandoned observatory on the plains holds the **Basic Rocketry** disk. → [Abandoned Observatory](observatory)
7. **Make fuel.** Turn crude oil into RP-1 and water into liquid oxygen. → [Oil Refining](oil_refining), [Cryogenics](cryogenics)
8. **Suit up.** Never go to space without all four EVA suit pieces and full oxygen tanks. → [EVA Suit](eva_suit)
9. **Build and launch your rocket.** → [Your First Flight](first_flight)
10. **Land on the Moon, build a base, come home.** Disks from the Moon's ruins prepare you for Mars. → [The Moon](moon)

> [!NOTE]
> No page in this book is locked. If a chapter starts with a **Research needed** note, learn that technology before you can put the chapter into practice.
