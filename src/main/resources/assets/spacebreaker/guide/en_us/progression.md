From your first bauxite to the Moon and Mars, step by step. No page in this book is locked; a **Research needed** note at the top of a chapter tells you which technology you need to put it into practice.

## 1. Bauxite and aluminium
Red-brown {item spacebreaker:bauxite_ore} is thin everywhere and rich in warm hills. Smelt raw bauxite in a furnace into {item spacebreaker:aluminum_ingot}. Two ingots stacked make a plate by hand. → [Ores](ores)
{recipe spacebreaker:aluminum_plate_by_hand}

## 2. First power
The lab runs on energy. Your first power is the {item spacebreaker:combustion_generator}: it burns coal or wood.
1. Stack two copper ingots: a **copper plate**.
2. A plate and **shears** make copper wire. The shears are not used up.
3. Wire, redstone and an iron plate make a {item spacebreaker:basic_circuit}.
4. Build the generator with the circuit.
{recipe spacebreaker:copper_wire_by_hand}
{recipe spacebreaker:basic_circuit}
{recipe spacebreaker:combustion_generator}

> Your first machine, the {item spacebreaker:crusher}, needs an {item spacebreaker:electric_motor}. Make the iron rods the motor needs by hand: three iron ingots in a column give two rods.
{recipe? spacebreaker:iron_rod_by_hand}

## 3. Lab and samples
{recipe spacebreaker:research_lab}
Put the generator next to the lab. Make a {item spacebreaker:sample_kit} and use it on natural ground: you get an Earth sample. → [How Research Works](research_basics)
{recipe spacebreaker:sample_kit}

## 4. First technologies
Research these with Earth samples:
- {tech spacebreaker:metal_pressing}: the press; plates and wire get much cheaper.
- {tech spacebreaker:metallurgy}: arc furnace, steel and alloys.
- {tech spacebreaker:electronics}, then {tech spacebreaker:precision_manufacturing}: the fabricator.
- {tech spacebreaker:pressure_suits} and {tech spacebreaker:oxygen_storage}: EVA suit and tanks.

> A machine does not run if its owner does not know the technology that unlocks it. Recipes are open to everyone.

## 5. Fuel
- {tech spacebreaker:fuel_refining}: pumpjack and refinery → **RP-1**. → [Oil Refining](oil_refining)
- {tech spacebreaker:solar_power}, {tech spacebreaker:electrolysis} and {tech spacebreaker:cryogenics}: oxygen from water, **liquid oxygen** from oxygen. → [Cryogenics](cryogenics)

## 6. The observatory
The cellar of the abandoned observatory on the plains holds the **Basic Rocketry** disk and **Suborbital Flight** data. Go ready for its guardians. → [Abandoned Observatories](observatory)

## 7. The rocket
In order: {tech spacebreaker:launch_infrastructure}, {tech spacebreaker:liquid_fuel_engines}, {tech spacebreaker:staging}, {tech spacebreaker:vacuum_engines} (with the Suborbital Flight data). → [Your First Flight](first_flight)

## 8. Orbit and the Moon
1. Reach orbit: you get **Orbital Flight** data. Come home with the emergency beacon.
2. With that data, {tech spacebreaker:landing_systems}: heat shield, parachute, landing legs.
3. Land on the Moon, build a base and collect the disks in its ruins. → [The Moon](moon), [Ruins on the Moon](moon_ruins)
4. Moon samples and disks prepare you for Mars. → [Mars](mars)

## 9. Mars and the way home
1. Go to Mars with {tech spacebreaker:interplanetary_transfers}; bring a sealed suit and plenty of oxygen.
2. {tech spacebreaker:sabatier_process} and {tech spacebreaker:isru}: water from ice, oxygen from water, methane from the CO₂ in the air.
3. Liquefy the methane and oxygen; with {tech spacebreaker:methane_engines}, fuel your methalox rocket on Mars and fly home. → [In-Situ Resources](isru)
