## Building it
1. **Flame trench:** lay a 3×3 of {item spacebreaker:flame_trench}. The rocket stands on the middle grate.
2. **Pad:** ring the trench with 16 {item spacebreaker:launch_pad} to make a 5×5 square.
3. **Service tower:** just outside the ring, build a {item spacebreaker:service_tower} at least **6 blocks** high.
4. Fit a {item spacebreaker:crew_access_arm} and a {item spacebreaker:fuel_umbilical} on the tower, facing the pad.
5. Place a {item spacebreaker:launch_control_console} nearby.

{items spacebreaker:launch_pad spacebreaker:flame_trench spacebreaker:service_tower spacebreaker:crew_access_arm spacebreaker:fuel_umbilical spacebreaker:launch_control_console}

> [!NOTE]
> The **pad and trench are required** to launch. Tower, arm and umbilical show up in the console's checklist; the umbilical fuels the rocket and the arm lets the crew board.

Press **Check pad** on the console: missing blocks are marked with flames.
