In Spacebreaker you reach space with real engineering. Rockets obey the rocket equation, and every world has its own gravity, air and temperature. Before you can build a new machine you usually have to **learn its technology** first.

Keep this book with you. If you lose it, make a new one from a book and some {item spacebreaker:raw_bauxite}:

{recipe spacebreaker:guide_book}

## Using the book
- The **Contents** lists the topics: click one to open its chapters, then click a chapter name.
- Turn pages with the arrows under the book, the arrow keys or the mouse wheel.
- The coloured ribbons on the right take you straight to a topic.
- {color #1E5A9A}Blue{/color} text is a link: hover an item name to see it, click it to open that item's page.
- The search box at the top searches the whole book. Shortcut: **Ctrl+F**.

> [!TIP]
> Open the book at any time with {key key.spacebreaker.guide}. If no key is bound, pick one in Options → Controls → Spacebreaker.
