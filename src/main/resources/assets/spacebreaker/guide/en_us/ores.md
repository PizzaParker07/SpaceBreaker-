The metals of the space age wait deep in the Earth. You can mine all of them with a **stone pickaxe** or better.

## Bauxite → aluminium
{items spacebreaker:bauxite_ore spacebreaker:deepslate_bauxite_ore spacebreaker:raw_bauxite spacebreaker:raw_bauxite_block}

A red-brown rock made of small round grains. It lies close to the surface in warm hills and gives aluminium, the metal of every rocket, tank and panel. You can smelt raw bauxite in a furnace:

{recipe spacebreaker:aluminum_ingot_from_raw_smelting}

## Other ores
- {item spacebreaker:rutile_ore}: **titanium**. Deep and rare.
- {item spacebreaker:pentlandite_ore}: **nickel**. Middle depths.
- {item spacebreaker:spodumene_ore}: **lithium**. High in the mountains.
- {item spacebreaker:wolframite_ore}: **tungsten**. Deep.

Each also has a deepslate variant and a raw block.

> [!TIP]
> Put raw ore through the {item spacebreaker:crusher} first: besides one dust there is a **50% chance of a second**. An ore block mined with Silk Touch gives **two dusts** straight away. → [Processing](processing)
