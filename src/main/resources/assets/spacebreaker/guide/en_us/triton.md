A captured dwarf planet orbiting Neptune backwards, the coldest surface ever measured: dimpled "cantaloupe" ground, pink polar caps of nitrogen frost, and in the southern cap geysers throwing dark columns of nitrogen and dust high up, bent into streaks by the thin wind.

> {item spacebreaker:methane_clathrate} burns like coal.

{entity spacebreaker:plume_beetle}
**Plume beetles** ride the geysers and glide down.

While a geyser erupts, {item spacebreaker:plume_dust} rains down around it, a few blocks from the vent. Pick it up: it is the plume beetles' food, and it keeps coming back (only a handful lies around at once).

## Ruins
A crashed lander teaches {tech spacebreaker:cryovolcanism}.

**Geyser Watch Station:** on the southern polar cap, a low glass dome on steel ribs built right over a live nitrogen geyser. The vent is ringed by bars in the middle of the floor; an instrument mast with a light rises through the dome's crown. The plume sampling lab holds a {tech spacebreaker:cryovolcanism} disk, the store holds supplies. Two plume beetles guard it.

>! Mind the geyser: it throws you up when it erupts.
