The youngest-looking and brightest of Uranus' large moons: faintly blue-white ice plains with carbon dioxide frost, few craters, and long straight **rift valleys** with steep walls and flat floors flooded smooth by icy lava, a low ridge down the middle. Uranus hangs in the sky with its thin rings.

> The valley floors are {item spacebreaker:graben_floor_ice}: icy lava that once rose between the faults and froze. Mining an {item spacebreaker:ariel_ammonia_ore} gives {item spacebreaker:ammonia_crystal}.

{entity spacebreaker:frost_moth}
**Frost moths** roost under the valley overhangs.

## Ruins
A crashed supply lander teaches {tech spacebreaker:ice_shell_tectonics}. In the Uranus system the way leads from Miranda to Ariel: first {tech spacebreaker:vertical_exploration} on [Miranda](miranda).
