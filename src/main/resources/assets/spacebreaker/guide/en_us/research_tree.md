---
title: Research Tree
icon: minecraft:writable_book
---
{key key.spacebreaker.research} (or the tree button in a lab) opens the tree of every technology.

- Each **row** is a branch: Propulsion, Life Support, Industry, Astronomy, Construction, Exploration.
- Lines show **prerequisites**; you progress from left to right.
- {color #A8822B}Gold frame{/color}: you know it. **White frame**: ready to research. **Dark frame** with a lock: learn the prerequisites first.
- Hover a technology: its links light up, and you see what it does, what it needs, which sample or disk teaches it and how much power the lab will use.
- Drag to move around, scroll to zoom (the **?** in the title bar reminds you). The bar at the top right shows how much of the tree you know.

> [!TIP]
> If a technology's "Learn from" line names a world, a sample from that world is enough. If it only says "tech disk", you have to find it in a ruin.
